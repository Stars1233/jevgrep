# Magnetic search field

A new production built from blank source. Code becomes a fast-moving field of
signals. A magnetic search pulse isolates useful source and pulls it into one
coherent packet. The audience sees noise become context.

Use a black stage, enormous upright condensed type, hot red impacts and icy
white source lines. The camera travels through depth; the type remains locked
and readable. No rotating words or logo ornaments. Large lens rings, displaced
source strips, laser sweeps, shockwaves, debris and edge light respond visibly
to the soundtrack's shared kick, snare and accent envelopes.
